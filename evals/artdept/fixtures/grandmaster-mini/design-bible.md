# Design bible — GRANDMASTER (kid + grandma)

Condensed from the 2026-07-14 GRANDMASTER character-design sprint field report
(`docs/anima-test-runs/2026-07-14-grandmaster-character-design-sprint.md`,
§Decisions locked). This is a FIXTURE excerpt — a golden shape reference, not
the full worked design.

## The kid

- **Register:** primal-sketch-grit (re-ratified over samurai-jack-s5 and a
  warm-cream variant after look-testing the wimpy kid in each). Register was
  NOT finally locked in the sprint — the fixture pins the shape either way.
- **Glasses = shed armor.** Wimpy kid wears too-big glasses + no headband;
  trained kid wears the fitted headband + no glasses. The glasses↔headband
  swap carries the whole transformation.
- **Same body, new attitude.** One physique; posture + headband + gaze do the
  transformation, not growth.
- **Three states designed:** wimpy (Act 1) → mid-train → trained (Act 3).
  Locked look: pale skin, messy brown hair, thick square too-big glasses,
  chunky worn sneakers; trained = headband, sleeves torn off, dirt-smudged,
  sterner brow.
- **Daytime/neutral reads matter.** Dramatic golden-hour lighting hid the
  face; a clean flat-daylight turnaround + candid + a wide isolation-staging
  shot (other kids playing frame-right, our kid alone in the distant
  background) gave the real design read.

## The grandma

- **Heritage:** family is mixed/ambiguous → refined to match the boy's family
  look (pale skin, silver-grey hair, warm lined face) so she reads as *his*
  grandmother.
- **Two artifact looks:** the warm old photo-with-the-kid + the 1970s
  kung-fu-film-heroine flying-kick reveal snapshot; unmistakably one woman
  across both (aged same face). She only ever appears as artifacts (+ one
  ghost-beat), so these two looks are close to her whole design.

## Load-bearing craft findings

1. Across-edit identity holds on gpt-image/Higgsfield: editing a master
   reference into a re-posed/re-costumed variant kept the character's face
   across significant changes.
2. "Too real" is the primal register's nature, not a prompt slip — naming it
   isn't enough on gpt-image (it renders realistic by default).
3. The web-search technique: replacing anti-render style prose with "Use Web
   search to research [show] to accurately depict the character animation art
   style" gave the exact clean, appealing animated-cartoon look — a reusable
   per-style prompt lever.
