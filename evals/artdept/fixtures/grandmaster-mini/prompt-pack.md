negation. EDIT/COMPOSITE (with reference) = terse, only the change; the
reference carries identity AND style, so one style-agnostic prompt each.

## ① FRESH — 1. Boy — WIMPY full-body anchor

**— Primal-grit cartoon —**
```
Full-body character-design anchor of a short and small, slight 9-year-old boy, front-facing three-quarter view, standing in a relaxed neutral pose on a plain neutral background under even flat daytime light so the whole design reads clearly. He has pale skin, a round soft face, thick dark expressive eyebrows, and messy brown hair. He wears large thick square eyeglasses a size too big for his face that slide down his nose, and a plain clean slightly-loose pale t-shirt, denim shorts, and chunky worn sneakers with slouchy socks. His body language is timid as he looks at the camera. STYLE: a stylized 2D hand-drawn ANIMATED CARTOON in the raw hand-inked register of Genndy Tartakovsky's show Primal. Use Web search to research Genndy Tartakovsky's show Primal to accurately depict the character animation art style. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

## ② EDIT — 6. Turnaround sheet — REUSABLE (feed it any anchor: wimpy boy / trained boy / old grandma / young grandma)
```
Image 1 is the character reference. Keep this exact character, outfit, proportions, and art style unchanged. Redraw them as a character-design TURNAROUND model sheet: the same character shown from four angles in a row — front view, three-quarter view, side profile, and back view — standing in a relaxed neutral pose, with consistent height and proportions across all four, on a plain neutral off-white background under even flat lighting. Do not add any text, labels, or watermark.
```

## ③ COMPOSITE (2 refs) — 9. Old grandma + the boy → warm keepsake photo
```
Image 1 is the boy. Image 2 is the woman. Keep both of their exact identities, outfits, and the art style. Combine them into a single warm, tender keepsake photograph: the elderly woman seated with her arm around the small boy, both facing forward with gentle, loving expressions — an ordinary grandmother-and-grandson snapshot in a softly-lit backyard. The boy is small child-scale beside the seated adult. Stage the whole image as an old polaroid photograph with worn borders. Do not add any text.
```
