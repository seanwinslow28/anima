ACCESSION
the gallery.

The museum layer isn't downstream of the work — it is the room the work is judged in. Every candidate hangs alone on a white wall with a true tombstone label; the critic's read is a conservator's condition report; and approval is accessioning — the red dot, an accession number, a credit line that names the director's decision. Taste-keeping becomes what it already was: curation, with provenance.

Mood — Higgsfield renders, soul_2
How it serves the soul

The pipeline is the portfolio piece — taken literally. In this direction the museum stops being a capture layer behind the tool: the tool is the museum, composing itself as you work. Each accession writes its provenance — both states, the treatment note, the decision, the date — so when the loop ships, the exhibition already exists. The walkthrough is not generated after the fact; you watched yourself hang it.

The critic proposes, never decides — in the register of conservation. A condition report locates a finding on the work (a pin on the proper left arm), speaks controlled vocabulary ("active drift", "stable"), recommends treatment, and rates the state. It cannot acquire and it cannot deaccession; recommendation and decision live in different professions, and the interface keeps them there.

The human owns taste — as the acquiring eye. "Approve" is generic; every tool has it. Acquire is a taste verb: it says this enters my collection, under my name, in the permanent record. The red dot is one of the oldest, quietest signals in art — and it is the only red on the wall.

What it needs from the daemon
One optional delta — D7

The three surfaces bind to Slices 1–5 as-is: tombstones and condition reports render /frames/{n}/candidates (Em verdict, cites, reasoning); the salon wall is the approved set; accession numbers derive client-side (slug + year + frame sequence). D7 (optional, later): persist the accession record — number, credit line, decision timestamp — server-side on approve, so the museum build reads it instead of re-deriving. Cousin to D5's taste-memory: honest derivation from real signals, never invented.
