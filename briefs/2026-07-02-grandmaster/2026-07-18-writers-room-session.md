## 1. Research digest (keyed R1–R10; provenance tags used throughout)

- **R1 — Silence + one foley; drop-sound-then-snap.** Tartakovsky built SJ "burnt out on dialogue"; ep. 8 holds ~1:20 of burning-tree frames with no characters before snapping to Jack's face. ([Animation Obsessive](https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/the-visual-world-of-samurai-jack))
- **R2 — "We're making a song."** Quiet parts and loud parts; "my timing has become slower... I want you really looking at something." ([CBR](https://www.cbr.com/how-genndy-tartakovsky-embraced-silence-for-unicorn-warriors-eternal/), [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/influencers/primal-creator-genndy-tartakovsky/))
- **R3 — Bobe Cannon limited-animation grammar.** Clipped move → abrupt hold; characters felt through timing; two-pose switches are gags. ([Animation Obsessive](https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/the-visual-world-of-samurai-jack))
- **R4 — Gag taxonomy.** Deadpan terminal line ("No. Jump good."); repetition-with-variation (*Jack's Shoes*); 30-sec wordless business; one-scene absurd side characters (60+ designs/2 weeks). ([TV Tropes Funny](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Funny/SamuraiJack), [Jack's Sandals recap](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Recap/SamuraiJackS2E13JacksSandals))
- **R5 — The substitution loophole IS the aesthetic.** Robots bleed oil; the workaround became the style. Validates the candy-liquid geyser. ([Medium](https://medium.com/@antoniowrites/samurai-jack-episode-3-8ffa997c8776), [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/features/general/samurai-jack-season-5-genndy-tartakovsky-interview-sex-violence-1201888020/))
- **R6 — Background as character.** Scott Wills; "no green grass, no blue sky"; Lawrence-of-Arabia environment sequences are legal. ([Animation Obsessive](https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/the-visual-world-of-samurai-jack))
- **R7 — Passage of time = object decay + palette rotation.** Premiere training montage is ⅓ of the episode, wordless, rotating visual worlds; the gi tatters to just the crest; S5 numbering jumps +40. ([TV Tropes Premiere](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/WesternAnimation/SamuraiJackThePremiereMovie), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Samurai_Jack_episodes))
- **R8 — *Jump Good* = training-montage template.** Absurd teacher, chores-as-training, deadpan two-word payoff. ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Recap/SamuraiJackS2E1JackLearnsToJumpGood))
- **R9 — *Four Seasons of Death* (XLIX).** Seasons themselves as act structure. ([Fandom](https://samuraijack.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_XLIX:_The_Four_Seasons_of_Death))
- **R10 — The held-silence emotional beat.** S5 ladybug: one tiny living thing moving through a frozen frame. ([Wikipedia S5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samurai_Jack_season_5))

## 2. LOCKED DECISIONS

### 2.1 Title: **FIRST LICKS**
The dad's humiliation line, reclaimed. Triple meaning: called up first / getting your licks in / the exit lollipop lick. The line stays diegetic **exactly once** (the Act-1 call-up), verbatim as the title's payoff. Rule fallout: no other "lick"-adjacent dialogue; Act-3 keeps the kid's lollipop pluck as the only *deliberate* lick (see C4 flag, §4.3).

### 2.2 Opening: **T2 — Cold-Open Smash ("The Sacred Object")**
1. Letterboxed dawn backyard as mythic battlefield; the piñata hanging like a relic; slow push-in; one streamer ticking (single foley); hold ~2s past comfort (R1/R2). Optional reverent low-angle of Sparkle Horse's absurd face played dead straight (plants the Act-1 gross-up).
2. SMASH-CUT to camcorder POV mid-"Happy Birthday" chaos — audio clipping, timestamp. The violence of the cut is the joke.
3. POV inventory: girl princess-mugs (smile-switch grammar born here) → dad narrates his own greatness over footage that undercuts him (sagging bounce house) → mom wordlessly curates the party *for the lens* (rotates cake to its good side).
4. On-tape one-off: **the grill-tong gag** (§2.4).
5. The find: zoom motor whines onto the kid at frame edge, not performing; autofocus locks; party audio thins. Longest POV shot in the sequence — the camcorder accidentally doing its first real hold.
6. **The line (locked, Sean's):** *"Wh--... When did Linda get the sad clown statue?"* — kid looks at the lens — **"WOOAH!!"** (yelp = foley, not dialogue; one-line rule intact).
7. Exit POV to the kid's POV: the holder resolves ONLY as silhouette/backlit/neck-down — startle reads through the camera yank + pose-switch + too-fast pan back to the fun. **Camera-holder never seen — rule KEPT** (viz dev to prove the body-language read; if it fails, the fallback is revising the rule, not the beat).
8. CLICK → all audio dies → one full second of silence over the film's first composed shot of the kid (long Cannon hold) → world ambience fades up. The film's camera takes over and never leaves him.

**Pacing grammar:** POV shots 1–2s jittery, audio hot; film-camera shots 4–6s locked holds. The audience must *feel* the grammar change at the click (R1, R3).

**No clowns anywhere else in the film** — protects the Linda line's singularity.

### 2.3 Shared VHS treatment (design once, reuse in Act 2)
4:3 inside the widescreen frame, rounded corners, chroma bleed, timestamp font, clipped audio, tracking lines. Act-2 home movie = same spec + the glitch layer. The intro teaches the texture so the Act-2 glitch reads as a violation of something familiar. **Act-3 climax is NEVER seen through the camcorder.**

### 2.4 The on-tape one-off: **grill-tong gag** (Sean's)
Griller flips dogs with tongs → scratches crotch with tongs → pause → sniff → pause → resumes flipping → double-take at the camcorder → wide-eyed "Shit" face into lens. **Generate two cuts: with and without the sniff beat** (the sniff is the grounded-vs-sketch tipping frame). The tong-griller owns the sequence's single lens-acknowledgment; **the card aunt (A2) migrates to film-camera background post-POV, no lens awareness** — nobody caught it at all, which is darker. Both go to viz test.

### 2.5 Ninja-star origin: **the box's second compartment, found mid-montage**
Everything he needed was already hers; he supplies only the year. No forging (implies pre-training capability; fights VHS-warm texture). Fire imagery preserved via B10 (sharpening, benched for later look-pass). **Plant:** her 1970s photo shows her holding the same star mid-flying-kick — never mentioned, freeze-frame reward. The mid-montage discovery (B15) re-charges Act 2b and explains why star vignettes cluster late.

### 2.6 World rules (party texture engine)
- **Bootleg maximalism.** Everything at this house is expensive-looking and slightly off-brand; Sparkle Horse is the tell. Maximum spend, minimum taste. (Feeds viz dev free jokes: airbrushed banner of the girl slightly wrong, etc.)
- **Party 2 = same compositions, escalated rentals.** Pony corner → petting zoo, same framing. The escalation-in-identical-frames primes the one thing that changed: him. (R7 fixed-frame delta; also cheap — layout reuse.)
- **Moon-bounce lockout** (staging): dad kills the bounce house for his party-2 piñata ceremony; kids pressed against its netting like inmates. Free dread.

### 2.7 Doctrine bookkeeping (labels)
- **PLANT/PAYOFF pairs (not one-offs):** camera-mom + smile-switch girl (plant Act 1) → flash-flinch (payoff Act 3, C1). Optional: hustler brother (50¢ drinks party 1 → dunk-tank operation party 2) — offered, not locked.
- **RUNNER (locked earlier, scheduled):** neighbor + hose — confused (B2) → curious (B4) → invested (B5 window) → imperceptible nod after B8's petal pin, hose off, never seen again.
- **DEVICE (not a runner):** the boombox decays as the calendar — new → duct-taped → snow-capped → sun-bleached, same song (R7, the gi principle).
- **Sacred, untouchable:** the 7am mailman nod; the Act-2 turn no-joke zone; grandma's laugh as the only permitted voice.
