## Gate 2 — the why-did-he-train cold read · RUN 2026-07-19 · **PASS**

**Protocol.** Five fresh-context readers (the STRESS-TEST fresh-subagent
pattern; no repo access, no web) read a motive-neutral, events-only outline —
no "tribute," no "in her memory," no motive language anywhere; the events
include the full humiliation and the dad's string-yank, so the revenge reading
had every chance to win. Each reader answered three open questions in one
sentence each: (1) why did the boy train for a year? (2) what is the film
about? (3) how does the ending land emotionally? Classification of answers
into "for her / for himself / inheritance" vs "to get them back" was done
after the fact; readers were never offered categories.

**The outline shown to readers** (kept verbatim for re-runs): the 13-beat
events-only pass — cold open → camcorder inventory → competence plant →
ritual/face-plant → walk home → the room → the box (headband + 1970s photo) →
the inscription → the tape + glitch match-cut → headband tied → year montage +
second compartment → invitation → return/sever/geyser/lollipop → end card.
(Full text in this file's git history and reproducible from concept.md's
structure section with all motive language stripped.)

**Results (5/5 readers):**

| Reader | Why did he train? (classified) | Revenge read? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rise from the ashes — answer her preparation; become what she planted | No |
| 2 | Become the person she already knew he was; never again be face-down | Explicitly "self-possession rather than revenge" |
| 3 | Her hidden life as permission to rise; grief → becoming | No |
| 4 | Finish what she started; answer her message | Explicitly "inheritance — not revenge" |
| 5 | Rise from the face-plant and become what she secretly was; her legacy → his self-respect | No |

**Verdict.** "For her / for himself" beats "to get them back" **5–0**. The pass condition from
concept.md — cut deeper on revenge evidence only if "to get them back" wins —
is **not triggered**. Bonus signal, unprompted and unanimous: every reader
independently called out the end-card tape-over as the emotional gut-punch,
and readers grinned at the lollipop beat — the tribute register is carrying
even in outline form.

**Caveat, stated honestly:** these are LLM cold readers, not humans. They are
strong at surfacing an available revenge reading when one exists (the same
fresh-context pattern that found real story faults in the 2026-07-18
red-team); a human 5-reader pass at the boards stage (gate 2's original
staging) remains cheap insurance if Sean wants it, but the pre-boards signal
is clean.
