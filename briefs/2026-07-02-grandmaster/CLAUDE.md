## The piece in one breath

**FIRST LICKS** (~3:30–4:00): a timid, unnamed kid is humiliated at a piñata party
(the host dad yanks the string — his signature cheat). Reaching for comfort he finds
his grandmother's absence instead — and in her room, a taped-over VHS revealing she
was a warrior who trained him his whole life through disguised games. He ties on her
red headband and trains for a year in her memory, "like a small samurai," through
mundane NJ suburbia framed mythically. At the next party he takes the piñata down —
stick splits it, throwing star severs the dad's pull-string — and walks home with a
lollipop and her stick on his shoulder.

## The law (one-liners; full text in concept.md DR + the pack)

- **Register `primal-sketch-grit`, piece-locked:** HEAVY bold weight-varying near-black
  ink OVER the color, gritty painterly masses, stark value contrast. Never thin/uniform
  line, never a watercolor wash. Generation via gpt-image + the web-search-Primal STYLE
  clause.
- **NO CHAIN-EDITING (DR #31):** every plate = fresh, or ONE edit off a clean source;
  drifted output is discarded, never re-edited. Fresh environment plates feed the
  on-register `KEEPER` as an art-style-only reference.
- **Comedy engine (DR #22):** the joke is the KID'S logic, never an absurd world; epic
  framing on mundane reality — the gap is the comedy AND the beauty.
- **Anti-revenge (DR #6):** practice target = burlap sack / tire, NEVER a piñata effigy.
- **Grief subtle (DR #26):** the decaying boombox is the tribute; no sorrowful glances.
- **East-Coast NJ suburbs (DR #28):** deciduous trees, clapboard colonials, telephone
  poles, cracked asphalt; negate palm/cypress/stucco/desert in every prompt.
- **Time-of-day law (DR #27):** ordinary varied daylight by default; golden-hour/god-rays
  RESERVED for designated hero beats. Rain only where it earns dedication (DR #29).
- **Season order (DR #23):** END-SUMMER → FALL → WINTER → SPRING; blossoms ONLY spring;
  boombox decay = the season clock.
- **Seasonal wardrobe (DR #32):** kid re-dresses per season; the red headband NEVER
  comes off; glasses come off at winter. Outfit turnarounds: `KID-MID`/`KID-FALL`/
  `KID-WINTER`/`KID-TRAINED` (paths in the montage pack).
- **Multi-angle locations (DR #20):** a key location = master + reverse + per-mark
  angles + a placement map, never one establishing plate.
- **Motion start-pose (DR #30):** any still bound for Seedance = the STARTING pose,
  never the peak.
- **Seedance 2.0 rules:** lead EVERY prompt with the flat-2D-cel **"animated on twos"**
  anchor (no negation — Seedance ignores it); generate LONGER than the hold + trim;
  state target seconds upfront for held beats. Engine-Truth caveat: if the anchor
  flattens motion into running-in-place, the better-moving take wins.
- **Act-3 weapons (DR #19 — misread twice, don't re-misread):** STICK strikes the
  piñata; STAR severs the pull-string at the dad's grip, never targets his body.
- **Generation is path-based** (ChatGPT Desktop app has the filesystem): cite refs BY
  PATH and name them; never re-describe a designed character/location; composites
  inherit the location's light; no text/watermarks; 16:9.
- **Keeper-paths-only (DR #35):** new packs cite `keepers/` + newly-ratified outputs
  ONLY; retired/archived plates are never cited or edited. All backgrounds people-free;
  every location's multi-angle set lands BEFORE compositing; composites are their own
  Sean-gated phase.
