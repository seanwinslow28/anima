## The ideas (Sean's, verbatim intent)

Additions to the **Act-2 turn + montage** (and the keepsake-photo artifact, pack item #9). Two alternative structures were offered:

**Option 1 — the photo progression + the handoff.**
- Start with old Polaroid photos of the boy **as a baby → all the way up to his current age**, telling the story of the grandmother-and-grandson relationship over the years (alongside her 1970s vintage karate photo).
- The **final** photo (current age) is **her giving him the worn red bandana** — either **in the hospital** or **in her room surrounded by IVs.**

**Option 2 — a few photos + the VHS + the music cue (the leaner, device-driven version).**
- Just a few photos of them through the years to show the relationship, plus her **1970s vintage karate photo.**
- Then he walks over to the **VHS player** — on the tape it's **his grandma teaching him karate as a kid.**
- The whole **emotional and funny monologue** gets him pumped up and leads into his training session.
- We hear the grandma say to the younger version of himself on the tape: *"Here. This music will help you get into the right headspace for training"* (or similar) — **so the music that plays under the training montage is set up diegetically.** The training session is **cinematic, comical, and oddly beautiful at times.**

## ⚠ Conflicts with the CURRENT locked concept (surface these in the brainstorm — a deliberate decision, not a silent absorb)

The current `concept.md` + studio-brief non-negotiables (front-door STRESS-TEST-steelmanned, Sean-ratified) that these ideas would **revise**:

1. **"No dialogue from her, ever" / "She never speaks and is never shown giving instruction."**
   → Both options give grandma a **spoken line** and Option 2 has her **actively teaching/instructing** on the VHS. Direct reversal.
2. **"The reveal is evidence, not instruction… the training is entirely his own choice, not her instruction, not a lesson she left him."**
   → A VHS of grandma **coaching him** flips *evidence → instruction*; if she taught him karate as a kid, it also softens the **discovery** engine (he'd already know she was a warrior, undercutting the reveal).
3. **"Not saccharine — no eulogy, no speech about the loss, no funeral."**
   → Option 1's **hospital / IV bandana-handoff** leans against the no-deathbed line.

**None of these is a blocker — Sean owns taste and may be deliberately changing the piece.** The point is only that these are *story-core revisions*, so they must be made in a story session, recorded against `concept.md`, and propagated to Sam (script) + Bea (board) — not folded silently into an image prompt. The tone fork (Option 1 vs Option 2) is itself a real decision the brainstorm should resolve.
