# FIRST LICKS — Beat Sheet v1 (the full spine, for Sean's pass)

*Drafted 2026-07-23 in the Phase-1 writers' room, consolidated FROM the record — every
[LOCKED] tag cites canon (concept.md §Structure + Decision Record, writers'-room doc
§2); [PROP] = my proposed connective tissue; [POOL] = a slot Sean fills from the
sanctioned pools; [OPEN] = a named open pick. **Nothing here is locked until Sean's
pass says so** — this sheet exists to be marked up: per beat, call KEEP / KILL /
REWORK / BRAINSTORM.*

**Logline:** Weeks after losing his grandmother — his best friend — a timid kid
humiliated at a birthday piñata ritual discovers she secretly trained him his whole
life through the games they played; he trains a year in her memory and returns to
take the piñata down like a small samurai.
**Theme:** Inheritance — grief-work as training; the world doesn't change, he does.
**Tone:** Sincere with a dark comic edge; samurai gravity welded to suburban banality.
**Runtime target:** 3:30–4:00 (stopwatch gate #1; montage trims first, the turn never does).
