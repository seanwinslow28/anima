## Stopwatch table-read protocol (Sean, ×2)

Read every SCRATCH VO line **aloud, 1952-serene pace** (unhurried — this is also calibration for R4), and act each silent beat in real time (count the holds honestly: the swivel gets its full "past comfort" duration; the replay gets its full circling). Phone stopwatch, lap button at each mark below. Run it twice; two passes that disagree by >5s at any mark means the read pace, not the sheet, is the variable — do a third and trust the pair that agree.

**Lap marks (10):**
1. ALARM #1 (end of beat 3)
2. Detonation (end of beat 4)
3. Hollow bell (end of beat 9)
4. ALARM #2 (end of beat 10)
5. Swivel START (start of beat 12)
6. Swivel END — after the full held beat
7. **QUESTION complete** (end of beat 13) ← **the 60s bar**
8. Replay end (end of beat 14)
9. Pit stop end (end of beat 16) ← **≤5s from its start**
10. Sting / black (beat 20) ← **total ≤2:00**

**Pass bars:**
- [ ] Question complete ≤ 0:60 on both passes
- [ ] Swivel held full duration (not rushed to make the 60s mark — if these two bars fight, the sheet loses, not the held beat: report it and we re-cut M1/M2)
- [ ] Replay ≥ 5s
- [ ] Pit stop ≤ 5s
- [ ] Total ≤ 2:00
