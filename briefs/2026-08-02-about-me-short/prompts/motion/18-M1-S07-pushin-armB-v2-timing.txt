1950s theatrical cartoon slapstick, hand-drawn colored-pencil pencil-test animation on cream paper, held on twos with visible line boil.

The room holds dead still. The small television's dark screen flares white in one hard flash and the whole room jolts with it at once — the word PROBLEM! bangs on above a jagged line falling to the right, the mug hops, the darts shiver in the wall, the taped sketches flutter. Immediately the camera slams in on the television in one fast smear zoom, the room streaking past, and stops dead. The screen flashes PROBLEM! on and off, on and off, on and off, on and off for the rest of the shot. The flash, the jolt and the zoom all happen inside the first three seconds.

Every time it lights, the screen shows the same word PROBLEM! in the same plain capitals above the same falling line. The television, the bookshelf of binders and the taped sketches stay the same drawings in every frame.

Squash and stretch on every accent, smear frames through the fast arcs, snappy timing, elastic overshoot, comic energy.
