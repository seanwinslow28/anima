## 0. Relation to the concept's craft baseline — what the research sharpens and adds

The ai-guru concept's §style bible was pre-filled from memory ("wet, alive linework; self-colored line; thick rubbery outlines; flat unnaturalistic color, sickly greens; extreme close-up detail at gross-out beats; unhinged proportions; commit-don't-cut-away"). All of that survives; the research **sharpens five things and adds two** — the register is authored (when greenlit) against these, not the pre-fill:

1. **The single discriminator is the DUAL-RENDERING-REGISTER "gross-up," not just "close-up detail."** The load-bearing tell is a *hard cut* from the flat cartoon into a **hyper-rendered, near-photoreal painterly extreme close-up** of one grotesque detail — a full-frame gouache-style painting with continuous-tone modeling — held a beat past comfort, then cut away. No other 90s-Nick cartoon runs two rendering registers in one shot. This is the money axis (§5.5, §6); the concept under-weighted it as "detail at gross-out beats."
2. **"Sickly greens / flat unnaturalistic color" → a two-stroke palette: a desaturated grimy GROUND punctured by ONE lurid over-saturated accent** — not globally saturated. The base is grayed-olive / bruised-violet / nicotine-brown, mid-to-dark, low-saturation; the *one* diseased thing (ooze, sore, inflamed flesh) is the only chromatically loud note. "Sickly" is *muted-ground + single-lurid-spike + hue-turned shadow*, not global candy-saturation (§5.2).
3. **"Self-colored line" is real, but the bigger finding is a LOGICAL LINE-WEIGHT HIERARCHY.** Heaviest on the outer silhouette and the *undersides* of forms; secondary on design subdivisions; thinnest on tapering detail lines that die to a point in open space. Every line describes a form; a line placed *just to fence off a color* is a dead "xerox" line and is the anti-pattern (§5.1).
4. **"Unhinged proportions" under-describes it — the grammar is CONSTRUCTION-THEN-BREAK.** The distortion is a *volume-preserving deformation of solid, correctly-constructed 3D forms* (rigid cranium sphere + hinged elastic "balloon jaw" + eye-spheres in sockets). The grotesque lands *because* intact construction is being violated; the character must remain recognizably itself at the peak of the distortion. Random ugliness that breaks the silhouette is a failure, not the style (§5.4).
5. **The commit-and-HOLD is a hard-cut, held-past-comfort duration effect** — the discomfort is as much *how long you sit in it* as the image itself.

**Added (not in the concept):**
- **A. Figure/background split is a load-bearing structural attribute:** flat rubbery cel figures composited over **painted, moody, slightly-decaying backgrounds** kept low-contrast so the flat figure reads as the highest-contrast graphic element. The lush "gallery-worthy" world is the straight man to the crude bodies (§5.3).
- **B. The register natively encodes the ai-guru money-shot.** Its flat/even-lit/clean sub-register = the "manufactured, propped-up, over-lit" pole; its rendered/single-source-lit/grimy sub-register = the "accidental, raw, real" pole. The hard cut *between the two treatments IS the reveal* — the piece needs no new grammar for its texture-shift (§5.6). The two-faces switch (§5.7) is likewise native: same construction, deformation-state swapped on a hard cut.

## 2. Output 2 — the Cy block (draft)

Proposed **Example D** for [`pipeline/agents/prompts/cy-character-designer-context.md`](../../pipeline/agents/prompts/cy-character-designer-context.md) (added at authoring): three sample `IR.*` entries for the ai-guru kid + a four-paragraph risk-bible excerpt in the register's own vocabulary.

**Three sample `IR.*` (the load-bearing trio):**
- `IR.{char}.construction.forms-wrap-not-flat` — features are constructed *on* volumes (center-lines curve around the cranium sphere; eyes are spheres seated in sockets, not flat pasted ovals). A frame where features sit flat, or the silhouette dissolves under distortion, fails.
- `IR.{char}.distortion.volume-conserved-through-extreme` — at peak expression the deformed masses conserve rough volume (the jaw-balloon lengthens as it narrows; limbs thin as they stretch) while the cranium and identity features stay constant. The character is recognizably itself at the extreme.
- `IR.{char}.palette.grimy-ground-one-lurid-accent` — dominant fills sit in a grayed, mid-to-dark, low-saturation band; **exactly one** high-saturation lurid accent per beat carries the "gross/contaminated" focus. Pure bright primaries throughout, or everything-saturated, is a defect.

**Additional wire-ready `IR.*` drafts the Bible pass can draw on:**
- `IR.{char}.shading.shadow-is-hue-turned` — shadow shapes are a *different hue and ≥ equal saturation* to their fill (flesh → grayed-olive/violet, not gray-brown), never a flat darker value of the same hue. Value-only shadows kill the sallow/diseased read.
- `IR.{scene}.figure-ground.flat-over-painted` — characters render flat/graphic (rubbery cel); backgrounds render painted/textured with atmospheric *low*-value contrast so the flat figure is the highest-contrast element. Same rendering treatment on both = defect.
- `IR.{char}.render.grossup-is-dual-register` — the gross-out extreme close-up is rendered in a *radically more detailed, continuous-tone painterly* style than the base cartoon; hard cut in and out, held past comfort. A gross-out beat drawn at the same flat detail level as the rest fails the discriminator.
- `IR.{char}.expression.committed-and-held` — extreme expressions are fully committed and held (no timid half-poses, no eased mask-morphs); the two on/off-camera faces switch on a hard cut with identical underlying construction, only the deformation state swapped.

**Risk-bible excerpt (four paragraphs, for Example D):**
1. **Drift directions.** Three pulls: (a) toward **Rugrats/Klasky-Csupo** — flat single-register, muted-earthy, potato/kidney geometric-abstraction held on-model (kills the dual-register disgust); (b) toward **clean/glossy modern vector or anime** — uniform keyline, on-model consistency, sanitized surfaces, cut-away-before-it's-gross restraint; (c) toward a **globally rendered/photoreal frame** — applying the gross-up's continuous-tone realism to the whole figure instead of quarantining it to the insert. The register lives in the *tension* between a flat cartoon base and a rendered grotesque punctuation; lose either pole and it collapses.
2. **What the register can't do.** It is not for pretty/clean/aspirational looks; it is deliberately ugly-*cute*, not ugly-*failed* — the ugliness is designed (solid forms, clear silhouette, playful shapes), never broken-craft noise. It cannot hold identity across a Bible unless construction is treated as the invariant (the distortion is a treatment on a persistent armature). And its signature gross-up is the register's biggest **legal/ethical trap** (§7): the depicted grotesque figure must be an original design, never a specific existing character.
3. **Where references thin.** The self-colored-line *value* rule and the shadow hue-turn are reconstructions from craft doctrine + stills, not a quoted show-specific house rule (§9). The gouache-render mechanics of the gross-up ("wet" = specular ping + sheen band + drips) are analyzed craft, not an itemized artist statement. Child-proportion figures in this exact register have thin canonical reference — the kid is authored fresh on the cute-baby armature, proportions declared at Bible authoring (SF03).
4. **The three binary review checks.** (i) Does the frame carry the *possibility* of the dual-register gross-up (flat base that could hard-cut to a rendered ECU)? no → generic-ugly-cartoon drift. (ii) Is the character recognizably itself at the peak of any distortion (construction survived)? no → identity/construction fail. (iii) Is the palette a grimy ground with *one* lurid accent and hue-turned shadows? globally-saturated or value-only shadows → palette fail.

## 5. The look, by dimension (condensed; corrections folded)

**5.1 Line & contour.** Thick-and-thin hand-inked outline governed by a **logical weight hierarchy** (John K, *Inking Advanced pt 1*): heaviest = the overall silhouette of the pose, heavier still at the *undersides* of forms (jaw, feet — "gives the character weight"); secondary = design subdivisions (clothing edges, the hair-mass outline); thinnest = detail lines (wrinkles, folds), "thicker in the middle," tapering to a point where they die into open space. Governing law: *every line describes a form; there are no floating lines placed just to fence off a color* — the tidy uniform "xerox" outline is the named villain ("cold, harsh, itchy"). Interior/organic separations are **self-colored** (a darker value of the fill they border — a warm-brown edge on flesh, a dark-red edge on a tongue), a real ink-and-paint technique (colored lines were the one element still hand-inked after Xerox automated black inking), so the border reads as belonging to the meat, not stamped on it. The "wet, alive" quality = **line boil** (frame-to-frame contour wobble, exaggerable even on a hold) + a rubbery brush-feel silhouette. *Honesty flag: the self-colored-line *value* rule is a defensible genericized ink-and-paint technique, not a quoted show-specific John K doctrine (§9).*

**5.2 Palette & color logic.** A two-stroke system: a **muted, grayed, low-key ground that reads as unclean** — grayed-olive / bruised-violet / nicotine-brown in a mid-to-dark value (Scott Wills: "slightly grayed purple and olive green… slightly darker than mid-range," neutral so it doesn't compete) — **punctured by one lurid over-saturated accent** that carries the disgust (infected-green, raw inflamed pink-red, bile-yellow). Contamination reads *because it is chromatically louder than everything around it*; if everything is saturated, the disgust reads as nothing. Even "flat" fills carry a subtle two-hue gradient. **Shadow is a hue-turned, saturation-lifted shape, not a flat darker value** — skin shadows into a grayed olive/violet, which is what makes flesh read sallow/diseased (grounded in general painterly shadow craft + the show's fine-art-trained painters; §9). This is the sharpening of the concept's "sickly greens."

**5.3 Shading, texture & the figure/background split.** Baseline = **flat cel color with hard-edged shadow shapes**; depth comes from line and shape design, not rendering. **All the texture is quarantined into the gross-up insert** (pores, stubble, grime, mucus, veins, sweat) — texture is a *punctuation device, not an ambient property*; the figure stays smooth so the rendered insert hits harder. Three surface registers stack: **flat-cel figure → brushy-gouache painted background → hyper-rendered gouache gross-up insert.** Backgrounds are genuinely painted (gouache, textured board; drybrush/sponge), lush but kept **low-contrast and neutral-valued so the flat characters read as the highest-contrast graphic element** — the refined, slightly-decaying world is the straight man to the crude bodies. The gross-up ECUs are the background/storyboard painters (Camp, Wray) briefly rendering the *character* — which is why the look breaks so hard from the flat cel.

**5.4 Form & proportion — construction-then-break.** Distortion is a **controlled deformation of solid, correctly-constructed 3D form**, not a flat drawing of something ugly (John K, rooted in Preston Blair): draw the solid form first (cranium sphere, cylinders), wrap center-lines around it, seat features *on* volumes. The head splits into a **rigid cranium sphere (identity/bone)** + a **hinged elastic jaw/cheek/mouth mass ("balloon jaw")** + **eye-spheres in sockets**. Proportion grammar = maximal contrast on a **cute-baby armature** (big cranium, small body ~2–3 heads, low-set features) cranked to extremes: huge eyes with **tiny pinprick pupils** (high sclera-to-pupil ratio = manic/frightened/predatory), dominant jaw. The distortion rule is **"MAINTAIN VOLUME"** (squash one side / stretch the other; the jaw-balloon lengthens as it narrows) with the **cranium and identity features held constant** — so the grotesque reads as *this exact character, screaming*, and snaps cleanly back to rest. Ugliness is **designed** (solid forms, clear silhouette, playful shapes — "ugly-cute," "delighted to be ugly"), never broken-silhouette noise. *This is the load-bearing `identity_lock` idea: distortion is a treatment on intact construction, and the construction is what must survive it.*

**5.5 Signature tells (ranked).** (1) **The dual-rendering-register gross-up** — a hard cut from the flat cartoon into a hyper-rendered painterly grotesque extreme close-up, held past comfort — the single axis that separates this school from every neighbor; if a frame carries no possibility of that insert, it is not this register. (2) **Off-model, volume-preserving elastic distortion** — emotion physically deforms the body while construction holds. (3) **Rich painterly backgrounds + a grimy-ground-one-lurid-accent palette under the grotesque figures** — the beauty of the world makes the ugliness land. #1 is the money axis; #2/#3 corroborate and can be partially shared by a neighbor.

**5.6 Lighting & the money-shot texture-shift.** Base lighting is low-key, moody, source-suggested (dim, grimy, one lit focal splash). Gross-out/dramatic beats escalate to **hard single-source theatrical lighting** (deep shadows, one raw key). This maps *natively* onto the ai-guru money-shot: **manufactured/fake = flat + evenly-lit + tidier + higher-value clean palette + wide staged framing** ("the cartoon lying to you"); **accidental/real = rendered ECU + single raw source + deep saturated hue-turned shadows + grime + one lurid accent + held too long** ("the mask slipped"). The hard cut *between the two treatments IS the reveal* — the register encodes truth-value in the texture/lighting shift itself; the piece needs no new grammar.

**5.7 Expression & the two-faces switch.** Extreme, committed, **character-specific** expressions (never generic stock mouth-charts). The on-camera showman mask = cute-baby armature cranked to camera-ready: huge unblinking eyes + tiny pupils, cheeks squashed up into a too-wide grin, jaw-balloon stretched. The off-camera collapse = the volume released: droopy lids, larger/softer pupils, jaw-balloon sagging down, forms de-energized to rest. **Same cranium, same identity, opposite deformation, switched on a hard cut** — native to the idiom (the gross-up's abrupt register-jump is the codified case), so a mask that flips like a light switch is *stylistically correct*, not a shortcut. (Nuance to keep honest: John K downplays the *cheap* take — distortion serves emotion, not vice versa; the value is the committed, constructed, *held* extreme with real feeling behind it.)

## 7. The non-derivative rule (doubly load-bearing here)

Capture the **school**, never the cast, the frames, the person, or the satirized target. Style is not copyrightable; a specific character, a specific rendered expression, a restaged shot, a title/logo, and a real person's likeness are protected — and this school carries a **second** constraint the others don't: its most-documented creator has serious, factual real-world misconduct allegations, so the register is authored as *a school of grotesque cel animation*, **never a person**. His published *craft pedagogy* (construction, squash-and-stretch, line weight) and Preston Blair's method are freely-usable technique; his **name never appears in a production prompt**. Layer three: the ai-guru piece's own non-negotiable — every AI tool/creator it satirizes is an **original generic design**, never a real tool's recognizable logo/UI/look.

## 8. Timing-bible additions (for the piece's animatic/motion phases; informs Bea/Mo, not the still register)

1. **Limited-animation baseline, full-animation bursts.** Most runtime is economical limited animation; energy is spent on a few great pose-driven moments (John K's "first ~5 of the 12 principles" doctrine: Solid Drawing, Appeal, Exaggeration, Staging, Timing).
2. **The build → distortion → HOLD arc.** Calm flat baseline → escalating off-model distortion → *freeze on the grotesque hold* (past comfort) → release. The stillness of the hold is engineered against the elasticity of the build; each makes the other funny.
3. **Wild takes** (sudden extreme distortions, pose-to-pose so "each drawing pops") + **smears** to connect extremes + the **boil** (shaky held lines keeping a still drawing uneasy).
4. **The gross-up is a hard cut, held, then a hard cut out** — never an eased dissolve; the discomfort is a *duration* effect.
5. **The two-faces switch is a hard cut** on a single frame boundary (no eased morph), same construction, deformation-state swapped (§5.7).
6. *For a still-image pipeline:* the register's timing translates to *keyframes that are either a wild-take extreme OR a held gross-up*, with the boil reproducible as a non-clean contour even on one frame. *Honesty flag: no source confirms the gross-up inserts run on ones specifically (§9); framed as full-animation punctuation, not a verified frame rate.*
