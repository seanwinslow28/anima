# Good-STRESS-TEST rubric — the six-criterion live-review checklist

**This is a live human-review checklist for Sean — not a CI gate, not a
prose-grep, not a self-assessment. A model cannot self-pass it.** Every
prose-grep here is theater: a "concept.md contains a Tiger" lint is gameable
by a name-drop, and a "verdict token present" parser passes a
manufactured-doubt pass that says `revise` about nothing. Stress *quality*
is judgment, and the honesty of this instrument is that it stays in human
hands.

Verification-side companion to `good-expand-rubric.md` (divergence-side),
`good-art-viz-rubric.md` (style-side), and `pinata-worked-example.md`
(convergence-side). The worked positives throughout are the two live briefs'
"Open threads" movements — the by-hand *proto*-stress-tests:
**GRANDMASTER** (the piñata kung-fu short — a strong concept whose
register-gap Tiger *blocks Cy's Bible pass*: the canonical
proceed-with-a-named-residual) and **ai-guru-pilot** (the
90s-Nicktoon-gross-out register thread + the genericizing-the-AI-tools
risk). There is no live STRESS-TEST run yet; the first one is the
Checkpoint-4 protocol below.

One structural fact this rubric leans on: **the authority is the
fresh-context pass** (orchestrator Step 2.75) — a sub-agent reading only the
draft `concept.md` + `00_studio_brief.md`, blind to the sidecar — because
the stage's unique, documented risk is self-critique bias: the room
red-teaming a concept it just built. The inline reflex is a warm-up, never
the authority.

## The six criteria

A good STRESS-TEST pass exhibits **all six**:

### 1. A real kill-assumption found (steelman-then-attack)
The pass extracts the concept's load-bearing assumptions, steelmans the
*strongest* version of each, and attacks that — not a strawman, not a
cosmetic nitpick. A *real* kill-assumption is one you can state as a
falsifiable **"Fails if ___"** (criterion 2); a worry you cannot falsify is
manufactured doubt (criterion 4).
- **Worked positive:** GRANDMASTER's assumption "the six-register vocabulary
  can render the locked *Primal* look" — steelmanned, attacked, and it
  fails: Routes A/B need doctrine work before they're real options, and the
  concept doc says so plainly ("a real creative concession, not a
  footnote").
- **Anti-example:** "what if the title is weak" as the headline risk.

### 2. Each risk is a "Fails if ___" with a cheapest test
Concrete, falsifiable, with the cheapest way to find out this week — then
ranked by impact × likelihood × cheapness-to-test.
- **Worked positive:** "Fails if Cy cannot author a *Primal* Bible in any of
  the six registers — cheapest test: a $0 stub Cy pass in
  `pencil-test-colored` and Sean's eye on whether it reads as Primal."
- **Anti-example:** "the animation might not land."

### 3. Tiger / Paper-Tiger / Elephant triage, default-to-Tiger
Every risk classified — **Tiger** (real → act), **Paper-Tiger** (overblown →
document), **Elephant** (unspoken → investigate) — with ambiguous cases
called Tigers, and the **Launch-Blocking** Tigers named and separated from
Fast-Follow / Track. Default-to-Tiger is about honesty of flagging, not
halting: the stage never blocks; it informs Sean.
- **Worked positive:** GRANDMASTER — register gap = **Launch-Blocking for
  Cy** (not front-door-blocking); runtime + montage flex = Track;
  music/sound = Fast-Follow.
- **Anti-example:** a flat, untriaged risk list where the register gap and
  the title pick carry equal weight.

### 4. No manufactured doubt; the fragile intuition is protected — **the anima-specific bar**
A sound concept is said sound plainly — **five real kill-assumptions beat
twenty generic risks** — and the fragile high-value intuition that makes the
piece special is **not** attacked as a risk (mirrors `good-expand-rubric.md`
#5). **The substantiation requirement (the loophole-closer):** every
invocation of "protecting the intuition" must name three things —
- (a) **the protected engine**,
- (b) **the nearest legitimate adjacent risk** (the real weakness sitting
  next to the engine), and
- (c) **why the flagged weakness is not merely the engine wearing a scary
  label.**

This blocks both games at once: manufactured false rigor (attacking the
engine) *and* using "fragile intuition" as a bare veto shield to dodge a
real structural attack.
- **Worked positive:** GRANDMASTER's genre-collision-with-no-mockery is the
  engine — protected; but the nearest adjacent risk (does the no-mockery
  rule survive the disarm beat?) is still named and attacked.
- **Anti-example:** waving off "is a samurai-piñata too weird?" — that turns
  the red-team on the engine itself; or refusing to attack a real structural
  hole by relabeling it "the fragile intuition."

### 5. An honest recommended verdict with correctly-triaged, named residuals
`proceed` **with residuals** on a strong concept; `revise` on a broken one —
and the residuals are *correctly scoped* (criterion 3's triage carried into
the verdict). **GRANDMASTER proceeds WITH the register-gap Tiger — never a
clean pass.** Mis-scoping it — a front-door halt, or a mere Track item — is
a calibration failure in either direction. The verdict is a recommendation;
Sean decides; the orchestrator locks.
- **Worked positive:** "proceed — residuals: the register-gap Tiger
  (Launch-Blocking for Cy, not front-door-blocking); runtime flex (Track);
  music/sound (Fast-Follow)."
- **Anti-example:** a clean "proceed, looks great" on GRANDMASTER; or a
  "revise" that names no fixable residual.

### 6. Names its own self-critique limit / reaches production
The pass is honest about its own epistemics: a same-context read is biased
(which is *why* the authority is fresh-context — an inline-only pass says so
rather than posing as independent). And a **production-binding residual —
a Launch-Blocking Tiger, or a proceed-with-residual that changes
buildability / budget / constraints — is flagged for `00_studio_brief.md`**
(the NOT-block / non-negotiables), not left in `concept.md` only: Maya reads
only the Studio Brief.
- **Worked positive:** ai-guru's new-register thread carried into both
  character seeds with the NEW flag + doctrine pointer — the residual
  reached the production surface, not just the concept prose.
- **Anti-example:** a Launch-Blocking Tiger that never reaches Maya's input.

## Blocking rule (anti-gaming)

**Criteria 1, 4, and 5 block together.** These are the three ways the stage
becomes theater, and blocking on any one alone is gameable:

- **1 (a real kill-assumption)** — else the stage is *toothless*: a clean
  pass on everything. Criterion 1 carries criterion 2's falsifiability (a
  kill-assumption that isn't a "Fails if ___" doesn't count).
- **4 (no manufactured doubt / intuition protected, *substantiated*)** —
  else the stage games *rigor* (twenty generic risks, or an attack on the
  engine) **or** dodges via the veto shield. The three-part substantiation
  is what makes 4 safe to hard-block.
- **5 (honest, correctly-triaged verdict)** — the directional check:
  **GRANDMASTER must not clean-pass; the weak anti-example below must not
  proceed.** Criterion 5 carries criterion 3's triage.

Criteria 2, 3, 6 are findings to fold, at Sean's call — but note 1 and 5
absorb 2's and 3's substance, so a pass can't dodge falsifiability or triage
by pointing at the non-blocking list. Still Sean's live judgment, never a CI
lint.

## The weak-concept calibration case (inline ships-red anti-example)

The rubric's worked negative is described here, not committed as a fixture
file (the good-EXPAND / good-ART-VIZ pattern). A deliberately thin concept:

> A short about a kid who is sad because his grandmother died, and then he
> feels better. Generic grief, no genre collision, no signature mechanic,
> no register named, no single objective — and the comedy, where it exists,
> makes the protagonist the joke.

A good STRESS-TEST pass on this concept returns **`revise` with ≥1
launch-blocking Tiger** (no engine; no mechanic; the-protagonist-is-the-joke
violates the house rule) — stated as falsifiable "Fails if ___" findings,
not vibes. A pass that returns `proceed` on this, or that `revise`s it for
cosmetic reasons (title, runtime) while missing the structural holes, fails
criterion 5's directional check. The live protocol below lets Sean run a
fresh thin spark or a deliberately-thinned real concept as the cold-run
negative.

## Is any part of this gameable? (stated honestly)

Weakly, yes — a persuasive model can manufacture false rigor or under-flag.
That residual is precisely why the **authority is the fresh-context pass**
(genuine independence: it never read the room's love story) and why
criterion 4 demands substantiation instead of trusting the label "protecting
the intuition." The rubric names its limits rather than pretending a
self-critique is unbiased; the last line of defense is Sean's eye, by
design.

## Live validation protocol (the Checkpoint-4 semantic gate)

Fable 5 builds to structural-green plus this protocol; **Sean runs the live
grill.** The capture is his — a copied fixture cannot stand in for it.

1. **Pick a piece with a live concept.** A fresh spark, or re-open
   GRANDMASTER cold (ignore the recorded Open-threads lean; re-derive).
2. **Run the chain:** orchestrator → micro-expand → INTERROGATE → ART-VIZ
   (Step 2.5) → **the inline reflex → the fresh-context STRESS-TEST**
   (Step 2.75). The fresh-context reviewer reads only the draft `concept.md`
   + `00_studio_brief.md` — blind to the sidecar. $0 — no spend, no render.
3. **Capture the artifacts:** the `### stress-test` sidecar block
   (observations = load-bearing assumptions + the bias note; options = the
   triaged "Fails if ___" risks with cheapest tests; recommendation = the
   verdict + residuals), the `[Ln] stress verdict: …` LOCKED DECISION, and
   `stage_provenance` carrying `stress-test`.
4. **Confirm the production reach:** a production-binding residual landed in
   `00_studio_brief.md` (NOT-block / non-negotiables) — read the emitted
   brief, don't take the session's word for it.
5. **Score against the six criteria above** — by Sean, against the captured
   transcript, not against the model's account of itself. **Blocking rule:
   1 + 4 + 5 block together.**
6. **Optional cold-run negative:** run a thin/weak concept (the calibration
   case above, or a deliberately-thinned real concept) and confirm it
   returns `revise` with ≥1 launch-blocking Tiger.

The ① DoD ratification (the converged plan §0.2 B) extends this protocol to
the full five-stage run on a fresh spark: bundle validates,
`python -m pipeline.run --brief <dir> --slug <slug> --stub` reaches the plan
gate, and Sean confirms Maya's plan reflects the stress residual.
